Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2026.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device in order to recognise the device or remember settings. They can be set by our website or by third parties (e.g. analytics or advertising services).

2. Cookies on this website

We currently do not set our own cookies for tracking or advertising on this website. We use the following external services:

  • Google Fonts — for loading fonts. Google may set cookies or receive your IP address. More: Google Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — if we use Netlify for hosting and form processing, Netlify may use cookies for its own purposes (e.g. security, statistics). More: Netlify Privacy Policy.

If in the future we add analytics (e.g. Google Analytics) or other tools that use cookies, we will update this policy and, where necessary, display a consent request (cookie banner) before setting certain cookies.

3. How you can manage cookies

Through your browser settings you can delete cookies or block them from being set. This may affect the operation of some parts of the website. You can usually find the information in the “Privacy” or “Settings” section of your browser.

4. Related documentation

For the general processing of personal data (including data you send us through the form), our Privacy Policy applies.
