In production

Movyn

Role: Co-founder, full-stack and AI

Cafes, bars and restaurants often track stock by hand, which leads to inaccurate counts and slow reconciliation. As a co-founder I built an AI-powered inventory SaaS that reads invoices and delivery notes, processes unstructured data, tracks recipe-based consumption and uses a unified PCS measurement model to keep counts consistent. Hospitality teams see what came in, what was used and what remains, without spreadsheets or recounting.

Platforms & marketplaces

Gallebo

Working, launch pending

Role: Full-stack and marketplace

Gallebo is a European marketplace for general aviation where pilots and passengers share flight costs in line with EASA rules. I built a multi-tenant SaaS with separate access and permissions for pilots and passengers, Stripe Connect for payment splitting, Didit for KYC identity verification and Supabase RLS for role-based data access. Payments, identity checks, roles and protected data work together in one secure application while the experience stays simple. This is a joint project, the system works end to end, and launch is waiting on the legal entity.

  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Stripe Connect
  • Didit KYC
View project

WALAR

Role: Full-stack

WALAR (World Accuracy Landing Ranking) needed to move its scoring off spreadsheets into a platform athletes, organizers and the public can use. I built a web application with a React/TypeScript frontend and a Supabase/PostgreSQL backend. The scoring engine (jump-distance lookup tables, placement bonus points, multipliers for national team members, juniors and medalists) is calculated server-side in PostgreSQL functions and feeds a rolling five-year leaderboard with filters. It includes database schema design, role-based RLS, GDPR-compliant consent for public athlete data, CSV/Excel import tooling and an admin panel.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL

KCPT

Role: Full-stack, led delivery, backend and database

A UK fitness coaching business with 400+ paying members needed a better way for members to plan meals, track weekly progress and share updates with coaches. I built a web application with Next.js and Supabase: weekly meal plans, a daily success tracker, progress history and branded PDF export, plus an admin dashboard for coaches. AI & automation

Respondly

Role: Back-end

An aesthetic clinic needed to answer patient questions on WhatsApp instantly, without risking a wrong or hallucinated answer reaching a patient. I built a WhatsApp AI support agent: RAG-grounded answers from the clinic's real FAQ content, a mandatory human-in-the-loop approval layer (every AI draft must be approved, edited or rejected before sending) and AI tool-calling (MCP) so the model decides when to book an appointment. It includes webhook signature verification, idempotent message handling and full audit logging. No AI message reaches a person without a human decision.

  • Supabase
  • RAG
  • MCP
  • WhatsApp

Savio

Demo project

Role: Back-end

A finance SaaS needed to turn raw income and expense data into insight users would act on, not another spreadsheet. I built the AI analysis layer that detects spending patterns, flags financial risks and generates personalized natural-language recommendations from structured data. It is deliberately not a chatbot but an automated system triggered by real user data. The product includes a financial data model (income, expenses, savings, debt), Stripe subscriptions and role-based gating that locks AI features behind a Pro tier.

Document Intelligence

Demo project

06 // FAQ

Questions and answers.

What exactly does NMSolux offer?

Custom web applications and systems: internal tools for companies, client portals, MVPs. We define the scope, agree on the price and deliver. 6 months of bug fixing included.

How long does a project usually take?

Smaller MVPs and websites usually take one to two weeks. Larger products, such as member portals or marketplaces, take several weeks and are delivered in stages. The deadline is agreed before the start.

How is the price determined?

It is agreed before the start based on the scope, as a fixed price or hourly, depending on the project. No hidden costs.

What does “6 months of support” mean?

Bug fixing is included for 6 months after handover. It is not a redesign or new features, but fixing whatever does not work as it should.

Do you work alone or with a team?

I lead most projects myself (Neven). When needed, I bring in collaborators; you have one point of contact and one invoice.
